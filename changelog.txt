---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.1.0
Date: 12/11/2026
  Major Features:
    - Play The Cave with Multi-Team Support: every team digs its own copy of the cave.
    - The Cave asks this mod which team a player plays for (also while spectating another team),
      whether a team has started (MTS staged start) and whether it is paused.
    - Train interiors, shelters, gardens and child caves become the owning team's MTS surfaces:
      other teams are kept out, their production counts for the team, a pause freezes them and they
      go when the team does.
    - Map setting "Disband a defeated team": a team that loses its Main Train or shelter while
      others play on returns to the landing pen and its slot is free again. Off by default, and
      The Cave then resets the team in place.
  Info:
    - Needs Multi-Team Support 0.7.10 and The Cave 3.3.6, on Factorio 2.1.23 or newer.
    - Tested on a server with a joined client in The Cave's default mode, Train Interiors and
      Shelter Defense, with and without Space Age.
